How to buy an apartment at VILNYI

You buy an apartment at VILNYI through the complex’s sales office: the price is 1300 $ per m² of total area, paid in hryvnia at the NBU rate, either in full or in developer instalments for up to 17 months with a 30–50% first payment; military personnel and medical workers get a 5% discount, and the state eOselia programme is not available for this complex.

How to buy, step by step

  1. Choose an apartment: drawings, areas, floors and prices of all 462 apartments are on the apartment pages and in the 3D finder on the site. (Apartments · Choose an apartment in 3D)
  2. Contact the sales office (phone, WhatsApp, Viber, e-mail or the office: 23 Hrushevskoho St, Uzhhorod) or send a booking request on the 3D site — a sales manager will call you to confirm the booking and agree the details. (Contacts)
  3. Choose how to pay: 100% or instalments with a 30% or 50% first payment; military personnel and medical workers get a 5% discount.
  4. Get the draft contract and the construction documents from the sales office. (Facts)
  5. Sign the contract: the final price, area and payment schedule are set by the contract.

How to pay: in full or in instalments

From the developer, for up to 17 months. The first payment is 30–50% of the price; the balance is paid over the instalment period.

  • Full payment — A single payment of the full price of the apartment.
  • Instalments · 50% down — A first payment of 50%; the remaining 50% in parts over up to 17 months.
  • Instalments · 30% down — A first payment of 30%; the remaining 70% in parts over up to 17 months.

What discounts are available?

5% discount: For military personnel and for medical workers. Discounts cannot be combined.

Can you buy with the eOselia programme?

The state programmes “eOselia” and “eVidnovlennia” are not available for this complex.
