Where is VILNYI located?
VILNYI is being built at 4A Mykhaila Hrushevskoho St, Uzhhorod, Ukraine — at the corner of Hrushevskoho and Zankovetskoi streets in the Novyi district, about 2 km from the centre of Uzhhorod, Ukraine. Plot coordinates: 48.61133, 22.27685.
Who is the developer of VILNYI?
The developer is VILNYI Group (15 years of experience; projects QCC2, Q CITIUM and Habitat in Larnaca, Cyprus). The construction customer is ОК «ЖБК «ВІЛЬНИЙ», the general contractor ТОВ «ВІНЕРБУД» (per the public listing).
What are apartment prices at VILNYI?
One price for every apartment: 1300 $ per m² of total area, paid in hryvnia at the official National Bank of Ukraine rate — 58,321 ₴ per m² (rate 44.8625 UAH/$ on October 19, 2026). One-room apartments from 1,427,698 ₴, two-room from 2,268,104 ₴, three-room from 4,055,059 ₴.
Are instalments available?
Yes. Developer instalments for up to 17 months (1 year 5 months) with a 30–50% first payment, or full payment when the contract is signed.
What discounts are there?
5% for military personnel and 5% for medical workers; the discounts are not combined.
Can I buy with the eOselia mortgage programme?
No. The state programmes eOselia and eVidnovlennia are not available for this complex.
When will the VILNYI buildings be completed?
Planned dates: building 2 — Q4 2026, buildings 1 and 3 — Q3 2027, building 4 — Q4 2027. Status on September 15, 2026: buildings 1–3 under construction, preparatory works on building 4. The dates are planned and may change.
What apartments are there?
462 apartments in four buildings: 310 one-room (24.48–45.97 m²), 130 two-room (38.89–88.60 m²) and 22 three-room (69.53–122.50 m²). Ceiling height 2.8 m. Apartments are sold without renovation.
Is there parking?
Yes, underground parking beneath the courtyard: 150 car places, 29 tandem places (sold separately) and 4 motorcycle places, 76 EV charging stations. Parking prices on request.
Does VILNYI have a shelter and a generator?
Yes. A shelter is provided in the underground parking. A generator serves all buildings. Heating is autonomous, from a rooftop boiler house.
What is on the grounds of the complex?
A supermarket (about 1,200 m²) and a children’s play centre (about 1,150 m²) in the two-level retail part, the VILNYI SPA fitness club with a 5 × 10 m pool and a spa area, and a closed car-free courtyard.
What is the building made of?
Monolithic reinforced-concrete frame, ceramic block walls, mineral wool insulation. Plot area 0.896 ha.
Is there a building permit?
Per the public listing: construction works permit No. ІУ013240916905 of September 18, 2024, plot cadastral number 2110100000:21:001:0432. The sales office provides the documents.
