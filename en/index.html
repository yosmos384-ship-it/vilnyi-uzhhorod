On-site amenities

The complex has a two-level retail part, a fitness club with a swimming pool, a closed green courtyard and underground parking. Everyday errands can be done without leaving the grounds.

Supermarket on site

A supermarket of about 1,200 m² on the first level of the two-level retail part. Groceries are a few steps from your entrance.

Children’s play centre

The second level of the retail part is a children’s play centre of about 1,150 m² — entertainment for children right by home.

Fitness club with a swimming pool and spa area

A gym, a 5 × 10 m swimming pool and a spa area in the annex of building 3 — training and recovery without a trip across town.

Underground parking

150 car places, 29 tandem places (sold separately) and 4 motorcycle places on the underground level beneath the courtyard, 76 EV charging stations, two entry ramps and lifts from the parking to the residential floors.

Shelter and backup power

A shelter is arranged in the underground parking. A generator is provided for all buildings.

Closed car-free courtyard

A ground-level courtyard for residents only: greenery, walking paths, playgrounds and a barbecue area. Access control, video surveillance and a concierge in every building.

Purchase terms

Full payment or instalments from the developer for up to 17 months with a first payment of 30–50%. A 5% discount for military personnel and medical workers.

  • One price for every apartment: 1300 $ per m² — in hryvnias at the National Bank of Ukraine rate of October 17, 2026.
  • From the developer, for up to 17 months. The first payment is 30–50% of the price; the balance is paid over the instalment period.
  • 5% discount: For military personnel and for medical workers.

Location

VILNYI is being built at the corner of Hrushevskoho and Zankovetskoi streets, in a district with well-developed transport and social infrastructure. Schools, kindergartens, shops and a public transport stop are a few minutes’ walk away.

  • 20 minutes’ walk to the city centre, about 7 minutes by car to Narodna Square
  • Bus and minibus stop next to the building
  • Kindergarten No. 8 “Dzvinochok” — 4 minutes’ walk; lyceums No. 12, No. 15 and “Imidzh” 500–600 m away
  • Novyi shopping centre and supermarkets within 800 m; the Uzh river about 1 km, Bozdosh Park about 1.4 km

Frequently asked questions

Where is VILNYI located?
VILNYI is being built at 4A Mykhaila Hrushevskoho St, Uzhhorod, Ukraine — at the corner of Hrushevskoho and Zankovetskoi streets in the Novyi district, about 2 km from the centre of Uzhhorod, Ukraine. Plot coordinates: 48.61133, 22.27685.
Who is the developer of VILNYI?
The developer is VILNYI Group (15 years of experience; projects QCC2, Q CITIUM and Habitat in Larnaca, Cyprus). The construction customer is ОК «ЖБК «ВІЛЬНИЙ», the general contractor ТОВ «ВІНЕРБУД» (per the public listing).
How much does an apartment at VILNYI cost?
One price for every apartment: 1300 $ per m² of total area, paid in hryvnia at the official National Bank of Ukraine rate — 58,353 ₴ per m² (rate 44.8871 UAH/$ on October 17, 2026). One-room apartments from 1,428,481 ₴, two-room from 2,269,348 ₴, three-room from 4,057,284 ₴.
Are instalments available?
Yes. Developer instalments for up to 17 months (1 year 5 months) with a 30–50% first payment, or full payment when the contract is signed.
What discounts are there?
5% for military personnel and 5% for medical workers; the discounts are not combined.
